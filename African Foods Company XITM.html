IT Security Policy Guidelines

Essential safeguards protecting corporate assets and employee environments.

Password & Access Standard

  • Minimum of 12 characters combining uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
  • Mandatory rotation every 90 days. Never write or share credentials.
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is strictly required across all corporate cloud applications.

Workstation Safety

Always lock machines when stepping away (Press Win + L or Ctrl + Cmd + Q).

Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Framework governing the use of African Foods Company hardware and software.

Equipment Management

Company-issued assets are intended primarily for professional tasks. Minor, incidental personal browsing is permitted during designated breaks provided it remains safe and compliant.

Prohibited Actions

  • Deploying unvetted or cracked external software utilities without IT validation.
  • Connecting unauthorized personal storage media or USB drives into workstations.
  • Exposing company directories to consumer cloud hosting services.

Procurement & Requisition Workflow

Standard guidelines for acquiring fresh IT hardware or software licenses.

  1. Submit Ticket: Log request details through the internal IT ticketing framework.
  2. Budget Sign-off: Department supervisor confirms allocation feasibility.
  3. IT Evaluation: Review for compatibility and standards compliance.
  4. Fulfillment: Procurement, asset tagging, and device deployment.
